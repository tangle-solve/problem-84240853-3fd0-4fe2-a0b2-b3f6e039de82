### CHEM-000421: High-turnover elemental-germanium bromoalkylation with a closed inventory
- **Subfield:** Round 6
- **Open:** Develop mechanically activated elemental Ge for alkene bromoalkylation with measured recovery and losses. A proposed engineering milestone is at least 100 cumulative turnovers without continuous net consumption of the Ge inventory.
- **Status:** Guo and colleagues demonstrated activation and catalytic bromoalkylation in 2025, alongside separate Ge recycling experiments. The 100-turnover criterion with a complete inventory is a proposed assessment, not a reported record.
- **Type:** engineering
- **Why:** Establishes whether elemental Ge can function as a reusable process material.
- **Key works:** G. Guo et al. (2025), “Elemental Germanium Activation and Catalysis Enabled by Mechanical Force,” [DOI: 10.1002/anie.202421446](https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.202421446).
